Style guide

How the pieces fit

A bookmark is a card that shows its favicon, its title and its address. A folder is a tile on the start screen, in the Folders screen and above the cards of its parent, and a row in the sidebar alongside All bookmarks, Inbox and Trash. Inside a folder, the back link above the title takes you one level up, to the parent or to Inbox. Sheets are made of glass, and the palette blurs the page behind it.
