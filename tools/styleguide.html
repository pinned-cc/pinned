    Pinned style guide

    The tokens and components of the app, drawn by the app's own stylesheet and markup. Values in this page are read from app.css as it is served, so it stays in step with the code. Where a specimen is markup written in this page rather than built by the app, the source file is named beside it.

    Foundations

    Colour

    Graphite surfaces with one green accent and one danger red. Surfaces step up in lightness as they rise. Text is ink or weak ink, nothing in between. Each row lists the rules in app.css that use the token.

    Surfaces

    Ink and lines

    Accent and danger

    Folder colours

    Each folder colour is a pair: the hue (left) tints icons, dots and fills; the pale (right) is text set on a tinted fill. Top level folders take cyan, coral, lilac, lime, mint and rose in the order they were created, and their subfolders take the parent's. The Inbox is tray, Trash is bin, places such as All bookmarks are ink, and plain is the fallback. A class such as hue-cyan sets --h and --hp for everything inside it, so markup never carries a style attribute for colour.

    Interaction

    Ground and glass

    Save panel

    Type

    One family, Geist, with Geist Mono for addresses and tokens. Three weights: 400 for running text, 500 for labels and controls, 600 for titles and primary buttons. Sizes are named by role; line heights are a separate scale. Text in a field is never under 16 px, so iOS does not zoom into it.

    Line heights

    --lh-none is for one line controls, where the box height sets the rhythm. The ratios are for running text. The pixel steps hold stacked label pairs such as a title over an address. Every address line, 11.5 px mono under a title, uses --lh-14: on a card, a phone card, a list row, a sheet head and the Dashboard.

    Candidates to merge later

    • 11.5, 12.5 and 13.5 px sit between steps. 11.5 carries every address line and the small chips; 12.5 the tab count and the tooltip; 13.5 the sidebar rows, toasts and list titles. Each could move to its neighbour, but that shifts pixels, so they stay until it is decided by eye.
    • Addresses at 12 px: the palette and the Add to sheet set theirs one step up from the 11.5 px address line. One size would do.
    • One off line heights left as numbers: 15 and 22 px, 1.25 (the heading of the Share, API tokens and Add to sheets) and 1.5 (the How to save page).
    • Favicon letters scale with their tile, about 0.4 of its side (9 px on a 20 px tile up to 22 px on a 56 px tile). They are set beside the tile size, not from the type scale.

    Spacing

    Padding, margin and gap sit on a 2 px grid: --s-n is n times 2 px. The steps used most are 4, 8, 12 and 16 px. A few odd values remain as numbers: 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13 and 15 px. They are optical nudges, mostly to line an icon or a count up with text, and are worth rounding when a component is next touched.

    Layout

    Radius

    Surfaces round by size: a small part 6 to 8 px, a card 12 px, a phone card or field 14 px, a sheet 26 px. Small square boxes (icon boxes, favicons, key caps) have their own steps. Pills take half their control's height. A shape inside a bordered shape takes the outer radius less the border, written as calc(var(--r-xl) - 1px), so the two stay concentric.

    Elevation and lines

    Most surfaces are separated by a 1 px line, not a shadow. Shadows mark things that float: a card lifted under the pointer, the floating search on the phone, menus, sheets and the palette. The top edge highlight is the light catching a sheet's rim.

    Glass

    One calm ground sits behind the app, the share page and the other pages: three soft lights over --ground-base, a fixed layer that never scrolls. Grid cards and folder tiles carry no light of their own: a clear tint, a soft rim and a small shadow, no blur, so the ground's colour shows through each card where it sits. Anything that moves, is lifted, pressed, held or dragged is solid, and every card is solid while a drag runs. Where the browser supports backdrop blur, the panels over the ground blur it: the sidebar 42 percent over 24 px, the palette 72 percent over 32 px, sheets and menus 86 percent (sheets 94 on the phone), sheets of action rows 90. The floating plus on the phone and the search on the share page show 70 percent; on the phone the palette stays solid while it moves in and takes the blur once it has landed. The phone selection bar is solid. Without blur support every panel is a solid fill.

    Motion

    One curve for nearly everything, a softer one for sheets sliding in. Movement is short, from 120 to 320 ms, and only one thing moves per action. The save panel has three curves of its own: it settles in over 420 ms, draws a tick over 200 ms on Save, and fades out over 200 ms before its window closes. With reduced motion set, a rule at the end of app.css turns every transition and animation off; the palette keeps an 80 ms fade so it is clear it opened. The scripts check the same setting and skip their own animations.

    Durations in use

    Counted from app.css. The sheet drag on the phone also uses 200 and 220 ms from sheets.js, and the grid and list switch uses a 150 ms view transition.

    Icons

    A single sprite of line icons on a 20 px grid, stroke 1.5, round caps, drawn in the text colour. Insert one with ic('name') from util.js; it renders svg.i. The default size is 20 px; buttons and rows set it to 16 or 18 px with the icon tokens.

    Focus

    Keyboard focus is a 2 px accent outline 2 px outside the element, from :focus-visible, so a mouse click never shows it. A card shows the ring around the whole card when its title link has focus. The search fields use a soft double ring instead of an outline, and text fields turn their border accent with a faint ring.

    Control sizes

    Four heights. Desktop toolbar buttons are 36 px. Anything meant for a finger is at least 44 px, on the phone and on touch screens at desktop widths. Fields and the search are 48 px. The primary button in a sheet is 52 px.

    Components

    Bookmark card

    One card per link. The favicon, the title on one line, the address in mono cut at the start so the port always shows, and a strip with the folder's dot, its path and the date.

    • Use the grid card for browsing and the list row when there are many links to scan. The choice is kept per device and applies to every view.
    • Hover on the desktop shows the action bar (Share, Open, Trash, More) in place of the date and lifts the card 2 px. Touch screens have no hover; a hold selects instead.
    • Selected is the green edge and the round tick. While selecting, the tick shows on every card and the action bar does not. In the list the tick sits at the row's end and the date steps left of it, so the two never overlap.
    • In Trash the title is a button, never a link: a click offers Restore and Delete now.
    • Do not wrap a title onto two lines or drop the strip; the strip is how a card says where it lives.

    Folder tile

    A folder as an object: its icon in the folder colour, the name, and a short row of its newest favicons with a count of the rest. Shorter than a card, so folders and links never look alike.

    • Use the tile on the Folders screen and above a folder's links for its subfolders. Use the picker tile (.fmini) inside sheets.
    • Locked folders show a muted name and a lock where the faces were. Opening one asks for its password.
    • Drop target: while a card is carried over a tile, it lights in the accent. Trash lights in the bin colour.
    • All bookmarks is always the first tile of the Folders screen, in ink. Trash is a quiet row at the foot, never a tile.

    Sidebar rows

    Places and folders share one row: an icon box tinted with the row's colour, the name and a count. The current row takes the tint and the pale text.

    • Rows are 40 px, 44 px on a touch screen. From 720 to 999 px the sidebar folds to a rail of icons; the names stay for screen readers and show in a tooltip.
    • Folders show their subfolders only along the path to the open folder.
    • More folds Import, Export, API tokens, How to save, Log out and Source behind one row.

    Title bar and toolbar

    The title, its count, the share button on a folder, then the view's own controls on the right: Add, the grid and list switch, Sort, Select and, on a folder, the three dots.

    • Order is fixed: Add, layout, Sort, Select, then the folder's three dots. Trash has Empty trash instead of Add.
    • Below 1100 px the labelled buttons fold to their icon; the label stays as the accessible name.
    • Select turns into a pressed Done in place, in the quiet grey of any pressed button. Nothing else in the row moves.
    • The three dots hold only what acts on the folder itself, never on its links.
    • On the phone the title row is .ptbar and the view's controls sit on the view row (.vbar) below it, 44 px targets.

    Search field

    One search, one look: at the top on the desktop, at the foot of the phone within thumb reach beside the round plus. Same height, ground, border, icon and 16 px text; only the width differs.

    • The field opens the palette; the search runs there. While it runs, the field shows the query.
    • The key hint (.kcap) shows only where the pointer is fine, so never on a phone.

    Palette

    Cmd K or / on the desktop, a tap on the search on the phone. Recent opens first, then Views and Actions when the query names one (New folder, also on the N key), then matches grouped by folder.

    • The one blurred surface over the page on the desktop. On the phone it drops from the top and stops above the keyboard.
    • Arrows move the highlighted row, Enter opens it, Escape clears the query and a second Escape closes. Results stop at 40 with a line saying how many more.

    Buttons

    Few kinds, each with one job. The accent fill is for the single primary action in view.

    • Add (.add) is the primary action of a desktop toolbar. Save (.save) is the primary action of a sheet, full width, 52 px. Only one of either in view.
    • Quiet (.btn) for everything else in a toolbar. Warn turns its text and icon danger red; use it only where the action removes something.
    • Text buttons (.tbtn) for secondary actions in sheets, such as Cancel, Copy or Save to Inbox.
    • Icon buttons (.ib) are 44 px round targets with no ground. Give each an aria-label.
    • New folder (.nf-plus, from nfPlus()) is one control beside Folders in the sidebar and on the Folders tab: a 28 px circle filled with the raised surface and its hairline, the plus in weak ink, inside a 44 px target. Hover and focus brighten the plus; the focus ring sits on the circle. Quiet on purpose: the accent fill stays with the one primary add.
    • Disabled buttons fade to 45 percent (sheet rows and phone bar buttons to 40 percent) and keep their place.
    • Press: buttons scale to 97 percent while held.

    Fields

    48 px, 16 px text, a label above in the label style. The address field is a mono text area that grows with its content.

    • Focus turns the border accent with a faint ring. Errors show under the field in danger red, in a sentence.
    • In a sheet fields sit one step below the sheet's surface.
    • Do not use placeholder text as the label.

    Sheets and dialogs

    A bottom sheet on the phone with a grab handle; a centred dialog of 392 px on the desktop. Password dialogs (lock, unlock, remove lock) are centred at every width. Specimens below render as the current window width does, so open this page at phone width to see the sheet form.

    • Rows (.sh-row) come in rounded groups; a danger row sits in its own group at the end.
    • Close by the scrim, Escape, a swipe down on the phone, or the close button where there is one. Tab stays inside the sheet.
    • The primary button stays in view under a body that scrolls (the Save sheet's foot, the Add to sheet's bar).

    Context menu

    A right click on a card, the menu key on a focused card, or the three dots on a card. On the phone the same items open as a sheet.

    • Order for a card: Move, Copy, Share, Trash, a divider, then Open, Rename and Pin or Unpin. The first group matches the selection bar; the second is what the bar's More holds for one card.
    • In Trash: Restore, a divider, Delete now.
    • Keys: arrows, Home and End move; a letter jumps to the next item starting with it; Tab or Escape closes.

    Selection bar

    Selecting starts with Select, the S key, a hold on a card, a Shift click, Shift and an arrow key, Cmd A, or a marquee drawn on empty space. A Cmd click opens the link in a new tab instead; on the Folders screen it picks the folder. The bar takes the search's place; the title and toolbar stay where they are.

    • Order on the desktop: the count, Select all, Move, Copy, Share, Trash, More. The phone bar has the same actions in the same order after the count and All.
    • Folding: where the row runs short, buttons fold to their icon one by one, in this order: Share, Select all, Copy, Move, More. Trash keeps its word longest.
    • Folders on the Folders screen select the same way; the bar then holds Select all and Delete, which asks once with the count. In Trash it holds Select all, Restore and Delete.
    • Ends with Done, Escape, a click on empty space or a change of view.

    Toasts

    A short line at the bottom centre that confirms what just happened. At most three at once.

    • With Undo when the action can be taken back; it stays 6 seconds and Cmd Z runs it. Without, 2.4 seconds.
    • Words: what happened, in the past tense, such as Moved to Trash or Order saved. No exclamation marks.
    • Screen readers hear the same words through the one live region.

    Empty and loading states

    Calm, centred in the space under the title, and always with the next step. This is the one place the app centres text.

    • All sorted when the Inbox is empty, Nothing here yet on a fresh install or an empty folder, Nothing opened yet on the Dashboard.
    • The dashed tile closes every folder, the Inbox and All bookmarks, so adding is always one click away.
    • Loading shows skeleton cards that pulse; a failed load keeps the last cards dimmed under a notice with Retry.

    Small parts

    Counts, chips, the key cap, the switch, the segmented control and the tooltip.

    Phone tabs and layout

    Below 720 px the app is one column. The start screen has three tabs, Dashboard, Inbox and Folders; only the chosen one carries a shape, and the shape slides when the tab changes. Search and the plus float at the foot over a soft fade, within thumb reach.

    • Targets are 44 px everywhere on the phone.
    • Gutters are 18 px (--gut); cards sit two to a row, three from 520 px.
    • Settings on the phone holds what the sidebar's More holds on the desktop. Each row's icon takes the row's hue like the sidebar's icon boxes: plain, and bin for Log out.

    Login and Save page

    The small pages, live. Login is a single password field; the Save page is the Save sheet on its own, opened by the bookmarklet, the extension or the share sheet.
