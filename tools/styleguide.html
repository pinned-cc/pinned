Style guide

How the pieces fit

A bookmark is a card that shows its favicon, its title and its address. A folder is a tile on the start screen, in the Folders screen and above the cards of its parent, and a row in the sidebar alongside All bookmarks, Inbox and Trash. Inside a folder, the back link above the title takes you one level up, to the parent or to the Dashboard. The Dashboard shows the links opened last under small day heads, each tagged with its folder. Sheets are made of glass, and the palette blurs the page behind it.

Sidebar rows

System rows and folder rows are one component.

Title bar

Back link, title, count, share, Sort, Select and, on a folder only, the three dots. A view's own controls sit on the view; the three dots hold only what acts on the folder itself.

View row on the phone

Under the title or the tabs, where the first section label sat: the order in use with its chevron on the left (a fixed label where the view has a fixed order), the view's actions on the right, 44 px targets. The Folders tab carries its order and New folder.

Folders grid

All bookmarks is the first card, a folder card in ink with every bookmark's newest faces. Trash is a quiet row at the foot, always there with its count. With no folders yet, a dashed New folder card follows All bookmarks.

Selection

Holding a card and letting go starts selecting with that card ticked; a tap adds the next. With cards picked, the group actions sit on screen: in place of the search on the desktop and of the search pill on the phone. Nothing else moves: the title, the tabs and the toolbar stay as they are, and Select turns into the pressed Done in its place, quiet grey like any pressed button, which ends selecting; so do Escape and switching tab. The three dots hold what the bar has no button for: Open, Rename and Pin for one card, Pin or Unpin for several. A right click on a card opens the same items first, then Move, Share and Trash.

Locked folder

Shut, it shows a lock where the count sits and asks for its password; open, a lock follows the name and the title bar carries the lock button.

Dashboard

Recently opened, grouped by day: Today, Yesterday, Last 7 days, Last 30 days, Earlier. An empty group never shows. It follows the grid or list switch. Its cards select like every card: Select on screen, a hold, Shift and Cmd clicks, the marquee; a picked card wears the round tick and the green edge.

Search field

One search, one field: at the top on the desktop, at the foot on the phone within thumb reach, beside the round plus. Same height, corners, ground, border, icon, 16 px text and padding; only the width differs. The / key hint shows only where a keyboard is the input. The field opens the palette; while a search runs, the field shows its query.
